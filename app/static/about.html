Why we built it

Late deliveries cost companies money and customer trust. We wanted a model that warns the operations team when an order is placed, and one that is honest about what it can know at that moment. That is why we removed every column that reveals the outcome, even though keeping them would have made our scores look better.

What we used

  • Python, pandas, scikit-learn and XGBoost for the data work and models
  • SHAP for the explanations
  • Google Colab for training, and Git and GitHub for the project
  • FastAPI for the prediction service and this website
