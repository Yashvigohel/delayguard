Limitations

  • The check form does not ask for coordinates, product category or profit. Typical default values are used, so reasons based on them are indicative only.
  • Market and region are chosen independently, so a combination that never occurs in the data is possible.
  • The results come from one dataset. They show how the model behaves on it, not on other companies.
  • SHAP values describe the model's behaviour. They are not proof of what causes a delay.
