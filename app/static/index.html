Only what is known at order time

Columns that reveal the outcome, such as the real shipping days and the delivery status, were removed. Order status was removed too after a leakage check.

Every estimate comes with reasons

The three biggest factors behind each prediction are shown as SHAP values, so a person can judge whether the estimate makes sense.

Open about its limits

The model is strong for Same Day and First Class orders and weak for Standard Class. The Results page shows exactly where.
